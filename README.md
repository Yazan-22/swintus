# 🐷 Swintus

**Swintus** (Свинтус) is a fast, chaotic card game from Russia, similar in spirit to **UNO**. The goal is simple: **get rid of all the cards in your hand before the other players do.**

The game combines simple card matching with special action cards, unexpected attacks, and quick reactions.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective is:

> **Be the first player to get rid of all the cards in your hand.**

Players take turns placing cards that match the card currently on top of the discard pile.

You can usually play a card if it matches:

* 🟢 The **color**
* 🔢 The **number**
* 🐷 The requirements of a **special card**

The game becomes more chaotic when special cards are played.

---

## 👥 Number of Players

Swintus can be played by:

**2–10 players**

The game is especially fun with **4–6 players**, because there are more opportunities to attack and interrupt other players.

---

## 🃏 Setup

### 1. Shuffle the cards

Shuffle the entire deck.

### 2. Deal the cards

Each player receives:

> **8 cards**

Players keep their cards hidden from everyone else.

### 3. Create the draw pile

Place the remaining cards face-down in the center of the table.

This is the **draw pile**.

### 4. Start the discard pile

Take the top card from the draw pile and place it face-up next to it.

This becomes the **discard pile**.

The player whose turn it is now starts the game.

---

# ▶️ How to Play

Players take turns clockwise unless a card changes the direction.

On your turn, look at the top card of the discard pile.

You may play a card that matches the current card by:

### 🎨 Matching the color

For example:

```text
Top card: 🔴 5

You can play:
🔴 2
🔴 7
🔴 9
```

### 🔢 Matching the number

For example:

```text
Top card: 🔴 5

You can play:
🔵 5
🟢 5
🟡 5
```

So the basic rule is:

> **Same color OR same number.**

---

## 🤷 What if You Cannot Play?

If you don't have a playable card:

1. Draw one card from the draw pile.
2. If the drawn card can be played, you may play it according to the rules.
3. Otherwise, your turn ends.

---

# 🐷 Special Cards

Special cards are what make Swintus different from a simple matching game.

Different editions of Swintus may contain slightly different cards or rules, so always check the rule sheet included with your particular edition.

The following are common special cards.

---

## 🐷 Хапёж — "Grab"

**Effect:**

The next player must draw **3 cards**.

Example:

```text
You → play Хапёж

Next player:
⬇️
Draws 3 cards
```

This is one of the main attack cards in the game.

---

## 😴 Захрапин — "Snorer"

**Effect:**

The next player **loses their turn**.

Example:

```text
You
 ↓
Play Захрапин
 ↓
Next player ❌
 ↓
Following player ▶️
```

---

## 🔄 Перехрюк — "Reverse"

**Effect:**

The direction of play is reversed.

Before:

```text
Alice → Bob → Carol → David
```

After Перехрюк:

```text
Alice ← Bob ← Carol ← David
```

---

# 🎨 Полисвин — Color Change

The **Полисвин** allows the player to choose the color that must be played next.

For example:

```text
Current card: 🔴 4

You play:
🐷 Полисвин

You choose:
🔵 BLUE
```

The next player must now play a blue card or another card that is legally playable.

This card is particularly useful when you have many cards of one color.

---

# 👋 Хлопкопыт — Reaction Card

Some versions include **Хлопкопыт**, a reaction card.

When it is played, players must quickly react by placing their hand on the deck/table.

The last player to react receives a penalty according to the rules of the edition being played.

This turns the game into a quick reaction challenge:

> 🐷 **PLAY → REACT → PANIC → LAST PLAYER GETS PENALTY** 😂

---

# 🤫 Тихохрюн — Silence

**Тихохрюн** is a special card that introduces a temporary silence rule.

After it is played, the affected player must remain silent for the specified period according to the rules.

Speaking when you are not allowed to can result in a penalty.

---

# ⚡ Перехват — Interception

Some editions/rules also allow **Перехват (Interception)**.

If another player plays a card and you have an identical card, you may be able to play it immediately, even when it is not your turn.

Example:

```text
Top card:
🔴 5

Player A's turn

Player B has:
🔴 5

Player B:
⚡ INTERCEPT!
🔴 5
```

Player B effectively takes control of the play.

> Check the rules of your specific edition, as interception rules can vary.

---

# 🐷 The "Свинтус!" Rule

One of the most important rules is calling:

> **"СВИНТУС!"**

When you are down to **two cards**, you need to announce "Свинтус!" according to the rules.

For example:

```text
Your hand:

🔴 7
🟢 3

You are about to play 🔴 7.

Before doing so:

🐷 "СВИНТУС!"
```

If you forget to announce it and another player catches you, you may receive a penalty, commonly **drawing 3 cards**.

---

# 🏆 Winning the Round

The first player who gets rid of **all cards in their hand** wins the round.

Example:

```text
Your hand:

🟢 2
```

You play:

```text
🟢 2
```

Your hand is now empty.

🎉 **You win the round!**

---

# 🧮 Scoring

Depending on the edition, Swintus can be played simply as a **race to empty your hand**, or with a scoring system.

In the common scoring approach, the winner receives points based on the cards remaining in the other players' hands.

For example:

```text
Player A → 0 cards
Player B → 4 cards
Player C → 6 cards
Player D → 3 cards
```

The winner can receive points based on the value of the cards remaining in the other players' hands.

> **Important:** Scoring and the exact values of cards can differ between Swintus editions. Use the scoring table supplied with your deck.

# 🔄 Game Flow

The complete game loop is:

```text
        ┌─────────────────┐
        │  Deal 8 cards   │
        └────────┬────────┘
                 ↓
        ┌─────────────────┐
        │ Reveal a card   │
        └────────┬────────┘
                 ↓
        ┌─────────────────┐
        │  Your turn      │
        └────────┬────────┘
                 ↓
       ┌───────────────────┐
       │ Can you play?     │
       └───────┬─────┬─────┘
               │YES  │NO
               ↓     ↓
           Play card  Draw
               │     │
               └──┬──┘
                  ↓
          ┌───────────────┐
          │ Special card?│
          └───────┬───────┘
                  ↓
           Apply its effect
                  ↓
          Next player's turn
                  ↓
          ┌───────────────┐
          │  No cards?    │
          └───────┬───────┘
                  │
              YES ↓
             🏆 WIN!
```

# 🧠 Strategy Tips

Although Swintus is easy to learn, there is still some strategy involved.

### 1. Get rid of difficult cards

Don't wait until the end to discover that you cannot play your remaining cards.

### 2. Watch other players

If another player has very few cards, they are dangerous.

Use attack cards against them when possible.

### 3. Save powerful cards

Cards such as:

* 🐷 Хапёж
* 🔄 Перехрюк
* 🎨 Полисвин

can be extremely useful at the right moment.

### 4. Control the color

If you have many cards of one color, changing the active color to that color can give you more options.

### 5. Remember "Свинтус!"

Getting caught without announcing it can undo your progress very quickly.

---

# 🆚 Swintus vs. UNO

Swintus is often compared to UNO because both games use:

* Matching colors
* Matching numbers
* Special action cards
* Drawing cards
* Skip/reverse effects
* A race to empty your hand

However, **Swintus has its own cards, terminology, penalties, and gameplay mechanics**, so it should not be treated as simply "Russian UNO."
