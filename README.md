# Swintus

Swintus (Свинтус) is a fast-paced Russian card game based on matching cards and using special action cards. The main objective is to get rid of all the cards in your hand before the other players.

The game is similar in some ways to UNO, but it has its own cards, rules, penalties, and gameplay mechanics.

## Objective

The main objective of Swintus is to be the first player to get rid of all the cards in your hand.

Players take turns playing cards that match the card currently on top of the discard pile.

A card can generally be played when it matches:

- The color of the current card
- The number of the current card
- The requirements of a special card

## Number of Players

Swintus can be played by 2 to 10 players.

The game is particularly suitable for groups of 4 to 6 players.

## Game Setup

### 1. Shuffle the cards

Shuffle the complete deck.

### 2. Deal the cards

Each player receives 8 cards.

Players keep their cards hidden from the other players.

### 3. Create the draw pile

Place the remaining cards face down in the center of the table. This is the draw pile.

### 4. Start the discard pile

Take the top card from the draw pile and place it face up next to the draw pile.

This card starts the discard pile.

## Basic Gameplay

Players take turns.

On your turn, look at the top card of the discard pile.

You can play a card if it matches the current card by color or number.
